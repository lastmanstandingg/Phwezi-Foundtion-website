About Phwezi Foundation

Education, Training and Development

Phwezi Foundation for Education, Training and Development is committed to providing quality education and practical skills that prepare learners for further education, employment, entrepreneurship and responsible citizenship.

Through secondary education, technical training and vocational skills development, the Foundation provides opportunities for young people to develop academically, professionally and socially.
